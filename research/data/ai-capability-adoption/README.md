# AI capability and adoption: evidence and chart data

Research date: September 20, 2026. These files support the [executive report](../../../trends/03-ai-capability-and-adoption.html).

- `sources.json`: the 32 cited sources, versions, methods and limitations.
- `workflow-evidence.json`: version 3's deployment and workflow evidence, including observed results, company reports, targets, and the assumptions behind the illustrative process diagram.
- `metr-horizon-v1.1-2026-09-20.yaml`: raw METR data retrieved from https://metr.org/assets/benchmark_results_1_1.yaml on September 20, 2026. METR's accompanying time-horizon page was last updated May 8, 2026. Retained as a dated snapshot; retrieval date is not an evaluation date.
- `metr-selected-models.csv`: the six models plotted in Figure 1. Values are minutes of human task duration. `p50_low` and `p50_high` are the published 95% confidence limits; `p80_minutes` is the central estimate for the higher success threshold. Selected for a readable sequence across providers, not as a complete frontier or rank ordering.
- `chart-values.json`: values for the two survey charts, plus the explicitly hypothetical cost example. Survey values are transcribed from the linked source publications, not estimated from images.

The HTML includes its styles and charts, so the report itself can be read offline or shared as one file. Local data links need the accompanying repository. External citations need an internet connection.

## Updating the report

Preserve the old snapshot when adding new measurements. Check benchmark versions, survey questions, populations, observation periods and paper revisions before comparing numbers. Keep causal estimates, self-reports and forecasts distinct. Change the HTML and chart data together; update the source register and the research date.

This review is selective, not a systematic review or a pooled estimate. Abstract-only sources are marked in the source register. The report does not claim that dated observations describe September 2026 conditions. The cost example contains invented assumptions and must not be used as a market estimate.

Version 3 adds operational depth to the workflow cases, including DBS service metrics and implementation measures, IBM transactions, recruiting outcomes, engineering review coverage, and clinical documentation. It distinguishes evidence that a process changed from evidence that the change improved business results. The quote-to-job diagram is an analytical example, not a documented deployment. The recruiting diagram simplifies an actual experimental design.

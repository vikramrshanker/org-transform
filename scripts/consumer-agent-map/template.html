01 / Decision framework

Meaningful EBITA improvement, with a fast and technically credible path.

The unit of analysis is a technology area that can support several workflows across service businesses. Assess its representative scope, the economic mechanism and the range of complexity inside it. Reusability matters because it reduces engineering work and accelerates subsequent deployments.

__RUBRIC__
Common scoring basis. Scores assess a bounded, connected implementation using established components and human exception handling. They do not assume that the easiest feature represents the entire area, or that its most advanced variant must be built first. All three scores run from 1 to 5; higher is better. Weighted total = Σ(weight × score ÷ 5), out of 100.

How to interpret return

Net EBITA uplift comes from incremental contribution after fulfillment costs, reduced operating expense or credible avoided incremental hiring, less ongoing software, model usage, review and maintenance costs.

Freed time, scheduled appointments and faster collections are intermediate outcomes. Track implementation cost separately, distinguish first-year earnings from steady-state uplift, and use consistent accounting treatment. Do not assume exit-multiple expansion.

How to interpret uncertainty

Return confidence is Low without operating baselines. Speed and feasibility confidence are Medium where established components exist but an integrated implementation has not been verified. Scores are transparent judgments, not measured ROI or vendor benchmarks.

One point in return changes the weighted total by eight points. Differences below five points are too small to justify a decisive preference. Missing evidence is an open question, not an automatic score of three.

Common delivery assumptions and scope

First-build estimates assume two experienced full-time engineers, part-time operating expertise, authorized access to documented APIs or usable exports, and no foundation-model training. They cover a bounded connected product, not an enterprise platform. Economic observation begins after useful deployment and depends on fulfillment, staffing, renewal or close cycles. Data-access delays can dominate total elapsed time.

Workflow independence and data accessibility are included in technical feasibility. Reuse contributes to speed; it is not an additional score. Individual-business qualification, partner selection and pilot execution sit outside this assessment.

04 / Illustrative applicability

One technical area can serve many operating models.

Direct means the area has a clear conceptual connection to a recurring operating process. Selective means applicability is narrower, depends on the business model or carries more demanding scope. Neither label establishes unmet demand, easy integration or realized uplift.

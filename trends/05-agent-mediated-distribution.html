00 / Executive brief

Discovery is moving faster than delegated buying.

There is convincing evidence that AI interfaces affect search behavior and send customers to merchants. There is also evidence of real agent-initiated payments. The scale and economics of those two developments are not yet the same.

01

A citation may produce little traffic

Pew found clicks on traditional results in 8% of visits with an AI summary, versus 15% without. Clicks on a summary’s own source links occurred in only 1% of summary visits.[1]

02

Some arriving shoppers are valuable

Adobe reports retail AI referrals converting 42% better than non-AI referrals in March 2026. This compares different visitor groups; it does not prove the assistant caused the higher conversion.[2]

03

Growth is real; market share is missing

Shopify reports Q1 2026 AI traffic up 8× and AI-search orders nearly 13× year over year. Without absolute volumes, those numbers cannot establish dominance.[3]

04

Transaction infrastructure is taking shape

Google, Shopify, Walmart and payment networks are connecting discovery with catalogs, checkout and authorization. Merchant operations and user permission remain essential.[4], [6], [8]

Definitions. “AI-referred” means a customer arrives from an AI interface. “AI-assisted” can include research or comparison. “Agent-initiated” means software initiates a transaction under delegated authority. These categories overlap; none is a synonym for an unattended purchase.

Less traffic for some publishers. Better traffic for some merchants.

These findings can coexist. An assistant may answer a general question without sending a click, then refer a more decided buyer when a purchase requires a merchant. That is a plausible interpretation of the evidence, not a measured explanation. Publishers and sellers face different exposure even when the interface is the same.

The current evidence does not justify a forecast that agents will capture a specific percentage of all commerce by a fixed year. It does justify measuring the new channel now, with separate counts for visibility, visits, orders and net contribution.

02 / Platform cases

The customer interface can move while the merchant keeps the work.

Commerce platform / Q1–Q2 2026

Shopify: distribute the catalog, preserve the transaction system

8×year-over-year AI traffic growth, Q1
~13×AI-search order growth, Q1
Human checkoutincluded in AI-channel commerce

What happened

Shopify’s June account describes a shared catalog and integrations with AI surfaces. It identifies merchant-site checkout through an in-app browser for ChatGPT discovery and eligible direct checkout in Copilot. Inventory, tax, payment, fraud and fulfillment remain commerce responsibilities.[3]

What it suggests

The platform can make millions of merchants reachable through a common integration. A new company selling basic feed connection must compete with this distribution. More promising gaps would involve specialized product data, difficult exceptions or measurable conversion failures.

Evidence boundary. These are Shopify’s own metrics and status descriptions. The 13× figure is not a count of autonomous purchases. No merchant-level margin or incrementality study is supplied.

Retailer and platform / Announced January 2026

Walmart: bring the retail relationship into the assistant

Geminiproposed discovery interface
UCPannounced connection standard
Not disclosedcompleted-order volume or profit effect

What happened

Walmart and Google announced a Gemini shopping experience connecting Walmart and Sam’s Club assortment with the retailer’s account and fulfillment capabilities. The announcement presents a planned integration rather than an independently measured commercial result.[7]

What it suggests

A retailer with inventory, member benefits and delivery capacity can carry those advantages into a new interface. The assistant does not erase the value of a reliable physical operation. It may change who controls comparison and the initial customer interaction.

Evidence boundary. The case supports an architectural direction. It does not establish launch completion, incremental sales or how commercial terms divide value between retailer and platform.

Payment network / Announced December 2025

Visa: distinguish authorized agents from ordinary bots

Hundredspartner transactions completed
October 2025Trusted Agent Protocol introduced
Consentmust connect the agent to user authority

What happened

Visa reports completed partner transactions and a protocol intended to let merchants distinguish legitimate agents from malicious bots. Its Intelligent Commerce documentation describes payment credentials, authentication and controls for delegated buying.[8], [9]

What it suggests

Delegation creates a new operational question: did the software act within the user’s instructions? Identity, spending limits, transaction evidence and exceptions matter alongside recommendation quality. This offers infrastructure work, but also attracts incumbent payment providers.

Evidence boundary. Successful partner transactions do not establish population-scale reliability, a lower fraud rate or settled liability for every transaction type.

03 / The transaction chain

A product description is not a purchasable offer.

UCP describes a common interface for business capabilities such as checkout and product discovery, with separate payment instruments and handlers. Google’s documentation keeps the retailer as merchant of record. Its March update extends cart, catalog and identity functions. These standards reduce integration friction; they do not guarantee shelf availability, service quality or product suitability.[5], [6]

Where an ordinary business may need to change
Business recordFailure visible to an agentOperational repair
Catalog or service descriptionAmbiguous specification, incompatible part or unsupported claim.Structured attributes, stable identifiers and an accountable data owner.
Inventory or appointment capacityA recommendation cannot actually be fulfilled.Connect availability to the operating system; define reservations, expiry and substitution rules.
Pricing and termsThe quoted total omits shipping, eligibility, tax or a cancellation condition.Expose the complete offer and verify it again before commitment.
Authorization and order handlingDuplicate purchase, wrong account or action outside the mandate.Trace the approval, enforce limits and prevent duplicate execution.
After-sales serviceNo clear route to cancel, return, reschedule or resolve a dispute.Preserve the order record and define human escalation and responsibility.

For a small service business, the missing piece may be a truthful schedule and a clear scope of work. A machine-readable description cannot make a bespoke estimate instant or guarantee that an available technician is qualified. The opportunity is partly operational: make the promise precise enough that another system can safely act on it.

04 / Measurement

Measure completed, profitable demand.

Cloudflare’s crawl-to-referral metric divides identifiable crawler HTML requests by attributable referred HTML visits. It is useful for understanding the exchange between content access and traffic. It does not count citations, unique shoppers or incremental purchases.[10]

A measurement ladder for an AI distribution channel
StageMeasureCommon mistake
EligibilityCan the relevant system retrieve current information?Treating crawl access as a guarantee of recommendation.
VisibilityAppearance and correctness across a defined set of representative customer questions.Reporting an unstable, hand-picked prompt score as market share.
ArrivalAttributable visits or qualified inquiries, with missing attribution recorded.Equating all “direct” traffic or AI-assistant mentions with AI acquisition.
TransactionCompleted paid orders, cancellations and fulfillment.Counting created carts, recommendations or payment attempts as sales.
IncrementalityNew contribution compared with an appropriate counterfactual.Giving the channel credit for a customer who would have purchased anyway.
RelationshipRepeat orders, retention, support cost and permissioned customer contact.Optimizing first-order conversion while losing future customer access.

Google states that websites need no special additional optimization or markup to appear in its AI search features beyond the usual eligibility requirements. That is a useful check on claims that a proprietary “AI optimization” tag is mandatory. It does not make good product information unnecessary or guarantee visibility.[11]

A careful pilot would use stable definitions, compare similar products and include a control where practical. Track catalog changes, seasonality, promotions and platform changes. A before/after traffic rise during a platform rollout cannot by itself establish that a merchant’s optimization work caused it.

05 / Economics

High conversion does not settle channel economics.

Consider a hypothetical merchant with 1,000 monthly AI-referred visits, a 4% purchase rate, a $100 average order and $30 contribution per order before acquisition cost. The channel produces $4,000 revenue and $1,200 contribution. These inputs are assumptions, not Adobe’s measured conversion rate.

Illustration: what could support an ongoing channel service?
ScenarioMonthly contribution before service costAfter $600 monthly service cost
All 40 orders are incremental$1,200$600
Only half of 40 orders are incremental$600$0
All orders incremental; contribution falls to $20/order$800$200

Setup costs, platform fees and incremental returns would lower those outcomes further. A traffic-growth chart cannot tell us which row applies. A viable provider must either serve enough contribution profit, improve the economics by enough, or deliver at a lower cost.

The same distinction applies to a marketplace. If agents simplify comparison, a new intermediary may gain traffic but struggle to charge for a recommendation. Conversely, a provider that verifies stock, guarantees service or handles a difficult transaction can still supply value after discovery becomes cheap. Whether customers will pay for that value remains a segment-specific question.

06 / Opportunity hypotheses

The stronger openings sit close to a real transaction.

For the repository’s company ideas, a narrow transactional problem is more testable than “help every company be found by agents.” One example would be making a specialist distributor’s availability and compatibility data dependable enough for a purchase. Another would be enabling a service business to accept a qualified booking with explicit scope and exception handling. Both require proving demand and surviving platform bundling.

07 / What would change the view

Watch absolute adoption and the division of value.

Next evidence worth collecting
QuestionUseful evidenceThesis weakens if…
How large is the channel?Absolute visits, orders and contribution share by category and merchant size.Growth remains concentrated in a tiny or subsidized base.
Who controls the relationship?Actual contracts, account access, repeat-purchase behavior and support ownership.Intermediary costs rise faster than merchant benefit.
Can transactions be delegated reliably?Completion, unauthorized-action, cancellation, return and dispute rates on real orders.Exception handling outweighs saved effort or users repeatedly revert to manual purchase.
Does specialist integration pay?Paid deployment across comparable businesses, including upkeep after catalog changes.Each merchant is bespoke or platform features eliminate the need.

The near-term evidence supports channel experimentation and operational preparation. The longer-term company question is who can retain value when product discovery becomes easier: the interface, the merchant, the payment network, or a provider solving a transaction problem none of them handles well.
